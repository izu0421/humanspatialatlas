Human Spatial Atlas HSA

The Atlas: public human spatial transcriptomics, curated per sample. The Agents: the pipeline that keeps it current.Every sample is downloaded as a cell- or spot-by-gene matrix with coordinates and metadata harmonised to UBERON, MONDO, HsapDv and HANCESTRO. The agents find, fetch and curate it, and re-run as new data is deposited.
