Human Spatial Atlas HSA

Two things, built together: the Atlas — public human spatial transcriptomics, downloaded per sample as cell- or spot-by-gene matrices with coordinates and harmonised metadata — and the Agents that find, fetch and curate it, and keep doing so as new data is deposited.

Human Spatial Agents the pipeline that builds and maintains the Atlas

Scout13 lanes — GEO, Zenodo, figshare, Mendeley, CELLxGENE, 10x, HuBMAP, HTAN, ArrayExpress, HCA, vendor showcases and spatial aggregators — searched for human spatial deposits and queued as candidates.
CurateEach candidate is judged: is it human, is it spatial, are the matrices and coordinates openly downloadable? Only then does it become a dataset.
FetchMatrix and coordinate files per sample, streamed with resume and a throughput floor, archives unpacked, name collisions resolved.
StandardiseTen matrix formats read into one shape, coordinates located however the platform stores them, and every sample QC'd so failures are visible, not silent.
HarmoniseTissue, disease, sex, age, development stage and ethnicity resolved to UBERON, MONDO, HsapDv and HANCESTRO; sex also inferred from expression where the panel allows it.
DeriveGene-panel identity, bin size from the bin grid, coordinate scale in microns, platform corrected from file evidence rather than the depositor's label, and Visium HD segmented from bins into cells.
